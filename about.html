I am Chinese, Singaporean, and Canadian, having grown up in Singapore before moving to Toronto for university. My interest in differences in political and ethical systems across cultural contexts led me to getting my BA in International Relations and Bioethics from the University of Toronto, during which I focused on global health governance.

I continue to be interested in the intersections of ethics, medicine, healthcare policy, and multiculturalism. Happy to connect with likeminded others!
